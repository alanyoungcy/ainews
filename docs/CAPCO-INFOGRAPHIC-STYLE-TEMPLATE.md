# Capco AI Intelligence Infographic Style Template

This template distils the supplied Capco and consulting-infographic references into a repeatable visual system for the weekly AI communication.

## Core visual idea

Present one clear executive claim inside a structured editorial data system. The image model supplies atmosphere and abstract structure; the application supplies all exact copy, numbers, pins, charts, and provenance.

## Palette

- Deep teal field: `#003E40` to `#07575A` for research-led data pages.
- Ink/navy field: `#05070C` to `#071B4A` for strategic and capital-markets covers.
- Electric violet: `#6F2BFF` to `#B13CFF` for AI, compliance, and transformation energy.
- Signal accents: Capco gold `#FBBF24`, aqua `#66B3B0`, mint `#BEE8D6`, and restrained magenta `#C45BFF`.
- Type: white or very pale mint on dark fields; dark navy/ink on light chart surfaces.

## Composition

- Use a portrait 4:5 or 2:3 editorial composition for a one-page visual, or a 16:9 composition for the studio hero.
- Reserve a quiet upper-left or central-left zone for the deterministic headline and executive thesis.
- Build a visible modular grid with thin hairline dividers, framed data cells, and deliberate alignment.
- Use one dominant visual structure: a radial map, treemap, ring, dotted field, flowing ribbon, or three-tier operating path.
- Keep a clear footer band for source, date, legal clearance, and Capco watermark.

## Typography and hierarchy

- Headline: oversized, high-contrast display treatment; use a condensed uppercase sans for data-led pages or a refined serif for an editorial macroeconomic page.
- Section labels: condensed uppercase sans, tight tracking, short phrases.
- Body copy: compact, highly legible sans with generous line spacing.
- Numbers: large, crisp, high-contrast numerals with a single accent colour for the most important metric.
- Use no more than three type scales in one visual: hero, module heading, annotation.

## Graphic language

- Hairline rules and framed modules are preferred to rounded cards.
- Use bars, rings, treemaps, numbered lists, maps, contour lines, and node networks as explanatory structures.
- Use dotted particle fields, topographic contours, radial systems, and soft flowing ribbons as abstract backdrops.
- Keep gradients controlled and directional; avoid neon rainbow effects, glossy 3D blobs, generic cityscapes, and stock photography.
- Use flags, icons, and logos only as deterministic overlays when the source requires them.

## AI backdrop contract

The image model must generate only the background system: texture, light, geometry, contours, particles, map-like fields, or abstract banking/technology atmosphere. It must not generate words, letters, numbers, charts, logos, flags, watermarks, or fake UI. The application overlays those elements deterministically from the grounded story and approved metrics.

## Capco guardrails

- Keep the visual practical, institutional, and decision-oriented.
- Anchor every key number to a source lock.
- Keep the Capco perspective distinct from reported fact.
- Preserve a source/footer zone and legal-clearance scrim.
- Prefer an editorial point of view over generic futurism.

## Prompt recipe

`[confirmed story] + [Capco perspective] + [selected archetype] + [one dominant visual structure] + [palette and texture direction] + [quiet overlay zones] + [no generated text/logos/numbers]`.
